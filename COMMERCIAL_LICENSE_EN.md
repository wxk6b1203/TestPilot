## Annex I: Contributor Profit-Sharing Program

### 1. Purpose and Scope

1.1 To reward developers who contribute to this project, the Licensor establishes this Contributor Profit-Sharing Program (the "Program"), under which eligible contributors (each a "Contributor") may share in a portion of the net profits derived from the software.

1.2 "Contributor" means a natural person or entity that has made contributions to the project (including code, documentation, design, protocol definitions, etc.), which contributions have been **reviewed and merged by the Licensor**, and who has completed the Contributor License Agreement (CLA) confirmation in accordance with CONTRIBUTING.md.

1.3 The specific ratios, amounts and time periods referred to in this Annex (including the Profit Pool percentage, calculation and payment period, minimum payment threshold and retention period) shall be **determined and publicly announced by the Licensor** from time to time based on business circumstances, and such determinations shall constitute a supplement to this Annex.

### 2. Shareable Revenue & Net Profit

2.1 "Shareable Revenue" under the Program means the aggregate of the following revenue derived from the software during the applicable distribution year:

- commercial license revenue (including license fees, subscription fees, support service fees, etc.);
- donations received on account of the software (including platform tips, sponsorship, etc.);
- revenue from commercial cooperation, joint development and similar arrangements based on the software;
- other revenue determined by the Licensor to be directly related to the software.

2.2 "Net Profit" means the Shareable Revenue less the following items:

- taxes and duties payable under applicable law;
- operating costs and expenses directly related to the software (such as servers and infrastructure, payment channels, legal and compliance, customer support, tool subscriptions, etc.);
- a reasonable operating reserve retained by the Licensor.

2.3 The Licensor shall retain financial records relating to the Program and shall provide a reasonable financial summary upon a Contributor's request.

### 3. Allocation Ratio and Contribution Assessment

3.1 Each year, the Licensor shall allocate a **certain percentage** of the Net Profit as a "Contributor Profit Pool" (such percentage to be determined and publicly announced by the Licensor) for distribution to Contributors.

3.2 Each Contributor's share shall be determined in proportion to their contribution level within the Profit Pool. The contribution level is assessed by comprehensively considering the quantity, quality, impact and ongoing maintenance of the Contributor's merged contributions, and shall be **reasonably determined by the Licensor** based on objective records (commit history, review records, etc.); if a Contributor disputes the assessment, they may raise it with the Licensor and the parties shall negotiate to resolve it in good faith.

### 4. Distribution and Payment

4.1 Distributions are made on an annual basis and shall be calculated and paid within a **reasonable period** after the end of each distribution year.

4.2 Payments shall be made in CNY (or another currency agreed by the parties for overseas Contributors); taxes borne by the payee under applicable law (including individual income tax and withholding tax) shall be borne by the payee, and the Licensor shall withhold and remit such taxes as required by law.

4.3 If a single distribution is below the **minimum payment threshold**, it shall be automatically carried forward to the next distribution; amounts that cannot be paid due to the Contributor's own circumstances shall be accumulated until payable.

4.4 Contributors shall provide valid payment account information; if the Licensor is unable to contact a Contributor within a reasonable period after the calculation under Section 4.1, the relevant amount shall be retained for a **reasonable period** thereafter, after which it shall be deemed waived by the Contributor.

5.1 If a Contributor breaches the originality warranties or other terms of the CLA, or engages in conduct that harms the project's reputation, the Licensor may suspend or revoke their eligibility under the Program; amounts already due and payable shall be unaffected.
